Google Search Keywords for Finnish Fence Companies
====================================================

Search these in Google and extract company websites + emails manually:

1. "aita yritys Suomi" (fence company Finland)
2. "aidat myynti Finland" (fences sales Finland)
3. "verkkoaita Helsinki" (wire fence Helsinki)
4. "puuaita Tampere" (wooden fence Tampere)
5. "metalliportti Turku" (metal gate Turku)
6. "aitaus palvelu" (fencing service)
7. "rakennusaita vuokraus" (construction fence rental)
8. "betoniaita myynti" (concrete fence sales)
9. "sähköaita myyjä" (electric fence seller)
10. "aitaelementit yritys" (fence elements company)

Finnish Business Directories (Free):
====================================

1. Finder.fi - https://www.finder.fi/
   - Search: "aita", "aidat", "aitaus"

2. Fonecta.fi - https://www.fonecta.fi/
   - Search: "aita", "portti"

3. Yellow Pages Finland - https://www.findagrave.fi/
   - Construction/Building materials section

4. Kauppalehti - https://www.kauppalehti.fi/
   - Business directory

5. Taloyhtiot.fi - https://www.taloyhtiot.fi/
   - Property management companies (often need fences)

4. Copy: company name, website, email
5. Add to your CSV file
